Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Short version: DockIntercept has no analytics and never sends your files anywhere. Buying and licensing are handled by Setapp.

Who we are

DockIntercept is a macOS utility developed by Maximilian Marquart (marquart.dev) and distributed through Setapp.

Data we collect

DockIntercept does not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no in-app accounts. Purchasing, licensing, payments, taxes, and first-line support are handled by Setapp (and its payment processor) under their own privacy policies — the developer does not receive or store your payment details.

How the app works

DockIntercept uses the macOS Accessibility permission solely to detect right-clicks on items in your Dock and to show its extended menu. All of this runs locally on your Mac. File paths, file contents, and your actions are processed on-device and are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Accessibility — required, used only locally to detect Dock right-clicks and render the menu.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. DockIntercept is distributed through Setapp, which is governed by Setapp’s own privacy policy. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Changes
